Frequently asked questions

What sites can you extract data from?

Public web pages: e-commerce catalogs, directories, listings, review pages, public registries and APIs. We do not extract login-walled data unless it is your own account and you authorize it, and we do not build personal contact lists for unsolicited marketing.

What format do I get?

CSV or JSON by default (Excel and Google Sheets open the CSV directly). Deduplicated, typed, with a short methodology note describing sources, run time and any caveats.

How do I pay?

By invoice after delivery: ACH or wire to our US business account. Card payments are being added. Recurring services (Price Watch) are invoiced monthly and can be cancelled by replying "stop".

Who actually does the work?

HireThomas, Inc. is a Delaware corporation. The extraction, normalization and quality checks are performed by AI systems the company operates, with a human owner responsible for the business. We say this plainly because you deserve to know how your data is produced — ask us anything about the method.

What if the data is wrong or incomplete?

You review the free sample before committing, and every Standard job includes one free re-run within 7 days. If a delivery does not match the agreed specification, we fix it or you do not pay.
