Method and limits

  • Source: each store’s public product listing; variant prices as listed (before tax, shipping and subscription discounts). Non-coffee items, bundles and samplers are excluded.
  • Price per ounce uses the bag size stated in the product or variant name; each store is summarized by its own median, so large catalogs do not dominate.
  • Stores were found through public directories. This is a broad sample, not a random or complete census, and it only includes stores that sell online.
  • Automated extraction can misread unusual size labels; implausible sizes and prices are filtered out, but individual figures may still contain errors.
