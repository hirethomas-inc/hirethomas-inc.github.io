Method and limits

  • Restaurants come from OpenStreetMap listings that include a website; regions are US census regions.
  • For each dish we take the first plausible price shown after the dish name on the menu page (add-ons skipped) and use each restaurant’s median, so one menu counts once per dish.
  • Automated extraction can pick up a neighbouring price or a different portion size, and menus online may be out of date. Treat single figures as indicative.
