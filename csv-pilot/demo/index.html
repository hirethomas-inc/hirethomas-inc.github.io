Rules used in this demonstration

  1. Trim leading and trailing whitespace in every data cell; do not change headers.
  2. Compare complete rows after trimming; remove exact duplicates and keep the first occurrence.
  3. Write a separate output, change ledger and audit summary. Do not overwrite the source.

Both transformations were explicitly enabled for this mock file. Customer files require agreement on the exact rules first. This global-trim method is not selected-column trimming.
